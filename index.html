Three on-chain moments

A tiny inscription.
A massive cultural spark.

BRC-20 gives fungible assets a simple rhythm built around deploy, mint, and transfer. The inscriptions are compact; the communities and histories they create can be enormous.

01 / DEPLOY

A ticker is born

Deploy gives the asset its identity, maximum supply, optional mint limit, and precision.

02 / MINT

The community joins

03 / TRANSFER

Ownership moves

A transferable inscription carries its amount to the first recipient it reaches on-chain.

Your keys, your moment

Know what moves before you sign.

BRC-20 balances depend on how inscriptions are interpreted and moved. A normal-looking Bitcoin transaction can still change the token outcome.

Keep it clear. Check the ticker, amount, available balance, inscription destination, recipient, and miner fee in an Ordinals-compatible wallet. A transfer is complete only after the transferable inscription reaches its intended recipient.
